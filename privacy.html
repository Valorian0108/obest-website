YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, in plain language.

This page explains how O-BEST Link Communication handles information you share through this website.

Information you provide

If you send an item request, we receive the item you are looking for, any device model or extra details you provide, your name if you choose to include it, and the phone number or email address you give us so we can reply.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

How we use it

We use your request and contact details to review what you are looking for, check availability or sourcing, and respond to you. Sending a request does not confirm a price, stock, or purchase.

Who handles your request

Your request is handled by O-BEST Link Communication. The website is hosted by Vercel, and request emails are sent using Resend to the shop’s email inbox. These providers process information as needed to host the website, protect it, and deliver the request. The shop may keep the email so it can respond and maintain its business records.

The website does not maintain a separate inquiry database. Service providers may process technical information, such as network and browser details, as part of operating and protecting their services.

Fonts and external links

This website loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google to retrieve them. Links to services such as Instagram and TikTok take you to those services, which handle information under their own privacy terms.

Your questions or requests

To ask about information you have shared with us, or to request access, correction, or deletion, contact the shop at 0810 546 3451. We may need to keep information where it is required for a legitimate business or legal reason.
