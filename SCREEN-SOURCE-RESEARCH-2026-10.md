# Phone-screen source research — 2026-10

**Status: research only / incomplete.** Nothing in this file is a customer-catalogue product recommendation. Do not copy records to `products.js`, publish them to Sanity, contact suppliers, or deploy based on this research alone. Supplier compatibility wording is a claim, not proof of fit. Product photos are supplier reference images; no image has been approved for the customer catalogue.

**Coverage at this checkpoint:** Lucent category-page titles were captured for TECNO (40 linked pages) and Infinix (40 linked pages, below the requested up-to-50 target), plus all 11 Samsung Note-category product URLs. Independent phone identity/display checks are recorded only for a subset; no supplier product photograph has been visually matched to independent handset/repair-part imagery. This is an evidence inventory, not a completed compatibility audit.

**Method note:** Lucent Parts category pages and their linked product titles were fetched directly. Independent web-indexed manufacturer/specification and repair-part results were used for model-name checks where noted. Text search results do not establish that a supplier's actual photographed flex/display assembly matches the phone; image-level/part-number/connector checks remain open unless explicitly stated.
